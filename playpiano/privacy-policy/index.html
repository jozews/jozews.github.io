🛡️ The Offline-First Promise

We do not want your data. Play Piano is designed strictly as a mobile and tablet application built to sit on your piano stand. It is an offline-first experience. We do not spy on you, we collect zero telemetry, and you can confidently use the core features of this app completely offline.

Privacy Policy

1. Zero Data Collection

Play Piano collects absolutely no personal or usage data. We do not require you to create an account, we do not monitor which pieces you play, and we do not use analytics, crashlytics, or background telemetry. We develop features based on common sense and musical necessity, not by tracking our users.

2. Third-Party Services (Google Play)

The application itself is self-contained. The only time an internet connection is utilized is to securely communicate with the Google Play Store to process or verify Premium purchases. All financial transactions and purchase data are handled entirely by Google's billing infrastructure. We never see, process, or store your credit card information, identity, or Google account details.

For details on how Google manages the data they collect during standard Play Store transactions, please refer to the Google Privacy Policy.

Terms of Service & Premium Agreement

1. The Premium Promise

Purchasing Premium status in Play Piano is a one-time commitment. Premium users are guaranteed full, unrestricted access to all currently available composers and sheet music, as well as all future music and composers added to the app. We will not charge you again for new content additions.

2. Offline Premium Usage & The 30-Day Cache

Because Play Piano is built to respect your privacy and function seamlessly on your music stand without constant internet access, your Premium purchase status is securely cached locally on your device.

  • You can use all Premium features completely offline for up to 30 consecutive days.
  • To refresh this cache, the app simply requires a brief internet connection to ping the Google Play Store and verify your purchase status. Once verified, your 30-day offline window resets automatically.

3. Platform Scope

Play Piano is currently developed exclusively for Android mobile and tablet devices. Due to the physical requirements of using digital sheet music at an instrument, the application is not designed for, nor supported on, desktop web browsers.
