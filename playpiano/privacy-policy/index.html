Privacy & Terms

Last updated: October 2026

Privacy Policy

1. Zero Data Collection

Play Piano collects absolutely no personal or usage data. We do not require you to create an account, we do not monitor which pieces you play, and we do not use analytics, crashlytics, or background telemetry. We develop features based on common sense and musical necessity, not by tracking our users.

2. Third-Party Services (Google Play)

The application itself is self-contained. The only time an internet connection is utilized is to securely communicate with the Google Play Store to process or verify purchases. All financial transactions and purchase data are handled entirely by Google's billing infrastructure. We never see, process, or store your credit card information, identity, or Google account details.

For details on how Google manages the data they collect during standard Play Store transactions, please refer to the Google Privacy Policy.

General Terms

1. Platform Scope

Play Piano is currently developed exclusively for Android mobile and tablet devices. Due to the physical requirements of using digital sheet music at an instrument, the application is not designed for, nor supported on, desktop web browsers.

2. Terms of Membership

For details regarding premium features, unlocking the full repertoire, and offline caching for members, please review our Terms of Membership.

🛡️ The Offline-First Promise

We do not want your data. Play Piano is designed strictly as a mobile and tablet application built to sit on your piano stand. It is designed to operate fully offline and without requiring a user login, and it plans to remain so. We do not spy on you, we collect zero telemetry, and you can confidently use the core features of this app completely offline.
