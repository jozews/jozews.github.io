🛡️ The Offline-First Promise

We do not want your data. Play Piano is designed to be an offline-first experience. We do not spy on you, we do not collect telemetry, and you can confidently use the core features of this app completely offline.

Privacy Policy

1. Data Collection

Play Piano collects absolutely no personal data. We do not require you to create an account, we do not track your usage, and we do not collect analytics, IP addresses, or device identifiers.

2. Third-Party Services (Google Play)

The only time the app requires an internet connection is to communicate with the Google Play Store to process or verify Premium purchases. All financial transactions are handled securely by Google's billing infrastructure. We never see, process, or store your credit card information or personal identity.

For more information on how Google handles your payment data, please refer to the Google Privacy Policy.

Terms of Service & Premium Agreement

1. The Premium Promise

Purchasing Premium status in Play Piano is a one-time commitment from us to you. Premium users are guaranteed full, unrestricted access to all currently available composers and sheet music, as well as all future music and composers added to the app. We will not charge you again for new content additions.

2. Offline Premium Usage & The 30-Day Cache

Because Play Piano is built to respect your privacy and function without constant internet access, your Premium purchase status is cached locally on your device.

  • You can use all Premium features completely offline for up to 30 consecutive days.
  • To refresh this cache, the app simply requires a brief internet connection to ping the Google Play Store and verify your purchase status. Once verified, your 30-day offline window resets.
