Terms of Membership

What you get when you become a Play Piano Member.

1. The Lifetime Unlock

Purchasing a Play Piano Membership is a single, one-time commitment—not a subscription. By becoming a member today, you are securing full, unrestricted access to the app for eternity.

This includes instant access to the entire current repertoire, allowing you to study and play the works of Gustav Mahler, Frédéric Chopin, Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart, Joseph Haydn, Ludwig van Beethoven, Johann Sebastian Bach, Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky, Felix Mendelssohn, and Richard Wagner. Furthermore, you are guaranteed access to all future music, features, and composers added to the app. You will never be charged again for new content additions.

2. Offline Premium Usage & The 30-Day Cache

Because Play Piano is built to respect your privacy and function seamlessly on your music stand without constant internet access, your Premium purchase status is securely cached locally on your device.

  • You can use all Premium features completely offline for up to 30 consecutive days.
  • To refresh this cache, the app simply requires a brief internet connection to ping the Google Play Store and verify your purchase status. Once verified, your 30-day offline window resets automatically.

3. Zero Data Collection

Your membership does not come at the cost of your privacy. Play Piano collects absolutely no personal or usage data. We do not require you to create an account, we do not monitor which pieces you play, and we do not use background telemetry. All financial transactions are handled entirely by Google's billing infrastructure. We never see, process, or store your credit card or identity data.

4. Platform Scope

Play Piano is currently developed exclusively for Android mobile and tablet devices. Due to the physical requirements of using digital sheet music at an instrument, the application is tailored for these environments and is not supported on desktop web browsers.
