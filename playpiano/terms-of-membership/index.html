Terms of Membership

What you get when you become a Play Piano Member.

1. Unlimited Repertoire

Play Piano members have full acess to the entire repertoire available and made available in future releases.

2. Feature Access & Future Capabilities

Play Piano members have access to all current features of the application, as well as the vast majority of future updates and standard enhancements we develop to improve your practice experience.

Play Piano reserves the right to introduce capabilities as separate, non-standard offerings. This specifically applies to features that may require server infrastructure, continuous maintenance, or recurrent costs to operate

3. Offline Usage

Play Piano is designed to operate fully offline and without requiring a user login and it plans to remain so.

Play Piano members will continue to enjoy the offline experience. The app may only require a connection to perform a cache refresh after more than 30 days of a device being offline.
