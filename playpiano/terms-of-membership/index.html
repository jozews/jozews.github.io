Terms of Membership

What you get when you become a Play Piano Member.

1. Unlimited Repertoire

Purchasing a Play Piano Membership secures your permanent, unrestricted access to the application's complete library of sheet music.

This includes instant access to the entire current repertoire—featuring works by Gustav Mahler, Frédéric Chopin, Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart, Joseph Haydn, Ludwig van Beethoven, Johann Sebastian Bach, Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky, Felix Mendelssohn, and Richard Wagner—as well as guaranteed access to all future music and composers added to the app. You will never be charged again for new sheet music content additions.

2. Feature Access & Future Capabilities

As a Member, you are entitled to all current features of the application, as well as the vast majority of future updates and standard enhancements we develop to improve your practice experience.

However, Play Piano reserves the right to introduce specialized future capabilities as separate, non-standard offerings. This specifically applies to potential features that require ongoing server infrastructure, continuous heavy maintenance, or recurrent operational costs to function. Should we introduce such features, your core membership will remain unaffected, and all offline capabilities, standard features, and repertoire unlocked by your membership today will remain yours permanently without additional cost.

3. Offline Usage

Play Piano is designed to operate fully offline without ever requiring a user login, and it will remain so indefinitely. Because the app is built to function seamlessly on your music stand without constant internet access, your Premium purchase status is securely cached locally on your device.

As a Member, you can use all of your unlocked features and repertoire completely offline for at least 30 consecutive days. This 30-day limit simply acts as a basic safeguard to process standard Google Play Store refunds. To reset this offline window, all you need is a brief internet connection so the app can quietly verify your purchase status in the background.
